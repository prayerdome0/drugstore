Our Story

From One Shelf To A Full Drugstore

SWDL Drugstore began as a small counter with a big idea: everyone deserves access to genuine, affordable healthcare products. Today, under the ownership of Seedwel Investment Limited, we operate a fully stocked modern drugstore at abc with thousands of products for sale.

The name SWDL carries our promise — Safe, Well-priced, Dependable and Licensed. Every product on our shelves is sourced from certified suppliers and checked by our pharmacy team before it reaches you.

  • Fully licensed pharmacy & retail drugstore
  • 4 departments: medicines, vitamins, skincare, first aid
  • Licensed pharmacists on duty every day
  • Proud member of the abc community
